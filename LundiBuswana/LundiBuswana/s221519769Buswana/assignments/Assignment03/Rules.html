Basic Rules

  1. Two players share one grid.
  2. Each turn a player writes either S or O in one empty block.
  3. If a player makes SOS in a row, column, or diagonal, that player scores a point.
  4. The player who forms SOS may take another turn.
  5. When the grid is full, the player with the most points wins.

Useful Note

The middle letter in SOS is always O, so players often watch the empty spaces around an O.
