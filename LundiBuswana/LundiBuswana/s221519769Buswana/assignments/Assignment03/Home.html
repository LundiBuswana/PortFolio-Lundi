About the Game

SOS is one of my favourite games because it is easy to learn and quick to play.

Players place the letters S and O on a grid while trying to form the pattern SOS.

The game looks simple, but you need to think ahead and block your opponent.

Why I Like It

  • Easy rules
  • Fast to set up
  • Good for strategy
  • Fun for two players
