Where I began

I come from a small town called Tsolo in Eastern Cape. I finished all my early schooling and matric at Lutuka, and then I took a brave step alone to Cape Town. Back then, I had never touched a computer in my life.

First choices

When I arrived in Cape Town, I was asked if I wanted Computer Science or Maths & Statistics. I had never used a computer, so I chose Maths & Stats. That choice gave me time to learn and grow without fear.

Graduation without the stage

I finally finished my BSc Maths and Statistics at UWC in 2022, but I never attended the graduation ceremony. The degree was officially conferred only in April 2024 because of finances, so this chapter feels both proud and bittersweet.

Finding my spark

In my first year at NMU I started to feel a new spark for computing and data. This was the moment I began to trust that my future could be built around technology and learning.

Second year

Fast-Forward in second year deep in Data Structures, where everything was starting to get more real where I could see again that my future is destined for the best.

Today

I am now in my third year at Nelson Mandela University, studying Computer Science and aiming for website development and data engineering. I am building real projects, one assignment at a time.

A calm story told clearly

This portfolio is written to let the reader listen. It shows how every assignment is part of my learning and every chapter is part of my journey.
