A story that begins in a small town

I grew up in Tsolo, Eastern Cape. I finished school and matric at Lutuka, then moved to Cape Town alone with no computer experience. My marks opened the door, and from that moment everything began to change.

Today I am a third-year student at Nelson Mandela University studying Computer Science and Data. This portfolio is a calm, honest, step-by-step record of that journey.

Why this portfolio exists

This portfolio is more than projects. It is the story of how a young student from the Eastern Cape learned to build websites and explore data.

Each page and every assignment here is a chapter in my learning, and every photo shows a moment on the path from Tsolo to Nelson Mandela University.
